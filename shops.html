What is a Featured Shop?

Greater visibility for local businesses

Featured Shop is MauMarket's optional promotional subscription. Sellers with an approved and active subscription may choose to display their shop publicly in Explore Shops.

01

Verified Seller

The business must first have an approved MauMarket seller account.

02

Active Subscription

The shop must have an active Featured Shop subscription verified by MauMarket.

03

Public Visibility

The seller must enable the option to display the shop in Explore Shops.
