Orders

How do I track my order?

Open your MauMarket account and check your order history. Order status may move through stages such as pending, verified, processing, out for delivery and delivered.

What if my order is delayed?

Check the latest order status first. If the expected delivery window has passed, contact MauMarket Support with your order ID.

Payments

Why is my payment still pending?

Some payments may require manual or automated verification before the order can continue.

Can I request a refund?

Refund eligibility depends on the order status, reason for the request and the applicable Buyer Policy.

Delivery

MauMarket coordinates platform delivery for supported orders. Buyers should make sure the delivery address and contact details are correct before fulfilment begins.

Buyer Help

  • Keep your account details accurate.
  • Review product details before ordering.
  • Use MauMarket support for disputes rather than arranging unofficial resolutions outside the platform.

Seller Help

  • Keep product information, pricing and stock accurate.
  • Process confirmed orders within the required timeframe.
  • Follow seller payout and Featured Shop requirements.

Account & Security

Never share your password or verification details. Contact MauMarket Support promptly if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.
