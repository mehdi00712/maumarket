Buyer Policy

This policy explains buyer responsibilities, payments, delivery, cancellations, refunds and disputes on MauMarket.

On this page

Ordering Payment Delivery Checking Orders Cancellations Refunds Buyer Conduct

1. Ordering

Buyers should review product descriptions, prices, availability, seller information and delivery details before placing an order.

2. Payment

Orders may remain pending until payment has been successfully received or verified by MauMarket. Buyers must provide truthful payment information and must not submit altered or misleading payment proof.

3. Delivery

Buyers are responsible for providing an accurate delivery address and reachable contact number. Where confirmation or signature is required, the buyer or an authorised recipient should be available to receive the order.

4. Checking Your Order

Buyers should inspect delivered items within a reasonable period and report significant issues promptly. Photos, messages or other evidence may be requested during a dispute review.

5. Cancellations

Cancellation may not be available once an order has entered processing, pickup or delivery. MauMarket will determine whether a cancellation can still be accepted based on the order stage.

6. Refunds and Disputes

Refunds are not automatic. MauMarket may review the order status, seller response, delivery evidence and buyer-provided information before deciding whether a refund, partial refund or other resolution is appropriate.

7. Buyer Conduct

  • Do not make fraudulent chargeback or non-delivery claims.
  • Do not abuse sellers, drivers or support staff.
  • Do not attempt to bypass MauMarket for the purpose of avoiding platform rules.
